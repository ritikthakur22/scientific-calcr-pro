Scientific CalcR Pro: Offline Scientific Calculator, Graphing & Symbolic CAS

## Full Description
Unlock the power of offline advanced mathematics with **Scientific CalcR Pro** — the ultimate scientific calculator, graphing toolkit, and symbolic algebra (CAS) emulator designed for students, engineers, and scientists.

Scientific CalcR Pro combines the authentic experience of high-end physical calculators (like fx-991EX ClassWiz) with an advanced 60 FPS graphing engine and symbolic math engine. Everything runs 100% offline, with zero ads and zero data tracking.

### Key Features:

*   **Natural Textbook Display:** Read and input mathematical formulas exactly as they appear in textbooks. Stacked fractions, square roots, and exponents are rendered beautifully on a dynamic 2D canvas.
*   **Symbolic CAS Engine:** Perform complex algebraic calculations offline. Simplify equations, factor polynomials, compute symbolic derivatives, integrals, and solve algebraic systems.
*   **60 FPS Graphing Engine:** Plot multiple functions concurrently. Pan, pinch-to-zoom, trace coordinate points, and find critical roots or vertical asymptotes instantly.
*   **Advanced Solver Suite:** Solve simultaneous linear equations (up to 4 variables) and high-degree polynomials (quadratic, cubic, and quartic) with complex roots.
*   **Matrix & Vector Algebra:** Perform dot products, cross products, transpositions, determinants, and matrix inversions (up to 4x4) with dedicated, high-performance engines.
*   **Numerical Calculus:** Compute numerical derivatives and integrations quickly using Simpson's rules.
*   **Privacy-First & Ad-Free:** Zero network calls, zero tracking, and zero ads. Safe for exam halls.
*   **Integrated Exam Mode:** Locks converter facilities and history logs for compliance with examination standards.
*   **Sleek Custom Themes:** Choose from Retro LCD, ClassWiz Blue, ES Plus Gray, OLED Black, and premium Light/Dark palettes.
